# COST — Buffett business memo

## Verdict: own the business

Costco shows the kind of durable earning power I want to own: revenue rose from $226,954 million to $303,154 million while net income rose from $5,844 million to $9,226 million. Owner earnings were positive in every reported fiscal year and grew from $3,501 million to $9,382 million, even after capital spending increased from $3,891 million to $6,435 million. That is a business producing more cash while continuing to invest in itself.

Returns on year-end equity stayed high across the record, ranging from 25.1% to 31.2%. The script's three Buffett checks all pass, so the financial evidence supports a business with consistent profitability and cash generation. I would own this business; I would watch whether that cash conversion and high return on equity remain durable, but the five-year 10-K record gives no reason to pass on the business itself.

This is a judgment about the business, not its market price or investment suitability.
